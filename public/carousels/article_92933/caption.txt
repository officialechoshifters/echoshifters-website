LIFESTYLE

Vogue World: Milano will celebrate Italian craftsmanship and human creativity in September 2026, marking the event's first stop in Milan during Milan Fashion Week. The global fashion celebration aims to highlight 'handmade artistry in the digital age' amid growing AI advancements.

The event will take place at Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, a historic glass arcade near Milan's Duomo and La Scala. Anna Wintour described the location as ideal for honoring Milan's fusion of heritage and modernity. The venue choice underscores the city's status as a hub where art, industry, and tradition converge.

Vogue World: Milano will feature thematic acts exploring fashion's evolution from Renaissance brocade to contemporary textiles. It will showcase collaborations between Italian fashion houses, master artisans, and international creatives. The program aims to demonstrate 'a continuous dialogue between hand and machine' in fashion production.

The event will support a €2 million investment to revitalize Quarto Oggiaro Library in Milan, transforming it into a cultural hub for creativity and community. Milan's city councilor emphasized libraries now serve as social spaces beyond book collections, aligning with the event's theme of preserving human craftsmanship in a digital world.

Vogue World's charitable initiatives have raised millions since 2022, but this edition uniquely ties its mission to local cultural infrastructure, showing how high fashion can directly support community development through creative partnerships.
