POLITICS & BUSINESS:

French education minister Edouard Geffray confirmed 400-500 high schools will stay closed Monday amid nationwide protests over overcrowded classrooms, teacher shortages, and unsafe facilities. The unrest has led to burned schools, 5,000 arrests, and 78 staff injuries — a record level of violence against education workers.

Protesters initially demanded better school conditions but turned violent as masked demonstrators set fires and clashed with police. The minister accused activists of infiltrating demonstrations and targeting educators, citing a case where a teacher was doused in gasoline during a Friday protest.

German Chancellor Friedrich Merz announced $1.5 billion in aid to Ukraine during a Kyiv visit, including $1.1 billion in military equipment for long-range strikes. The package aims to help Ukraine counter Russian drone attacks, which Zelenskyy says have reached 900 weekly strikes, with half intercepted.

Spanish authorities reported two deaths from flooding in Catalonia, with rainfall exceeding 200mm in 12 hours. Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez urged residents to avoid non-essential travel amid extreme weather warnings.

The French government faces mounting pressure as student-led protests demand systemic school reforms. Meanwhile, Germany’s military support package reflects deepening Western commitment to Ukraine’s defense capabilities amid escalating cross-border strikes.
