President Trump bars major news outlets from White House coverage amid growing GOP concerns over his policies. The move comes as midterm elections approach, with economic pressures mounting from high gas prices and the ongoing Iran conflict.

The White House cited security concerns for restricting access to CNN, The New York Times, and The Washington Post. This follows a week of escalating tensions, including a 200-day war in Iran and record diesel prices. Trump claims the conflict is driving up fuel costs but promises resolution soon.

California Democrat Rep. Ro Khanna pushed for international AI cooperation, urging Chinese tech firms to adopt guardrails similar to U.S. companies. He argued that self-improving AI poses civilization-threatening risks. Meanwhile, China plans $300 billion in state-backed data centers over five years, raising concerns about AI development aligned with human rights abuses.

Florida Republican Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar accused Trump of betraying Hispanic voters who supported him in 2024. Her campaign ad highlighted this tension as GOP leaders face pressure from constituents amid economic struggles.

The Republican Party faces mounting internal conflict as midterm deadlines approach, with voters directly impacted by policy decisions. Trump's approach to foreign policy and economic management appears increasingly at odds with his own party's base.
