She hinted at motherhood during a late-night talk show appearance in November 2024, calling it her next major role. The couple’s journey to parenthood includes a secret engagement, revealed when Gaga introduced Polansky as her fiancé at the Paris Olympics in July 2024.

Gaga’s recent tour, the Mayhem Ball, marked a significant milestone in her career. The global tour, spanning 12 months across five continents, grossed over $419 million and sold nearly two million tickets. Critics praised the tour for its theatricality and creativity, with NME calling it a showcase of her artistic vision.

The couple has remained private about the baby’s details, including name, gender, and birth date. While they have shared moments of joy publicly, no further information has been released. The announcement has sparked widespread excitement among fans, many of whom have followed the couple’s journey closely. Gaga’s transition from pop icon to mother represents a new chapter in her life, one that continues to captivate audiences.
