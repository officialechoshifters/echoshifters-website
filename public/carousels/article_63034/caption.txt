Lil Durk Acquitted of Murder-for-Hire Plot in LA Shooting

Lil Durk was found not guilty of murder-for-hire after a three-day jury deliberation in Los Angeles.
Prosecutors claimed he ordered a hit on Quando Rondo, which killed Rondo's cousin during a 2022 shooting.
Defense argued the real mastermind was his assistant, Kavon Grant, who sought to impress Durk.
Durk remains in custody over a separate racketeering charge related to his OTF label.
