HEALTH

Chad Gilbert, lead guitarist for pop-punk band New Found Glory, died peacefully in his sleep at 45 on September 20. His wife Lisa Cimorelli confirmed he was surrounded by loved ones during his final moments. The musician battled cancer for over three years, having been diagnosed with metastatic pheochromocytoma in 2021.

Gilbert underwent multiple surgeries including removal of half a liver and an adrenal gland. His condition progressed from adrenal cancer to Stage 4 adrenocortical carcinoma that spread to his brain. He received radiation treatment and spent months in hospital care before his passing. Despite health challenges, he remained active with his band until recently.

Bandmates described Gilbert as a creative force who shaped their musical evolution over nearly three decades. His contributions spanned more than a dozen albums and global tours. The band's statement highlighted his greatest pride as being a husband and father to his daughter Lily.

Fans and fellow musicians expressed overwhelming grief online. Dashboard Confessional praised his resilience while bands like Good Charlotte and The Offspring shared condolences. Gilbert's wife noted the community's support during his final hospital days.

His legacy extends beyond music—he also produced for bands including A Day To Remember and fronted hardcore side project International Superheroes Of Hardcore. Gilbert's journey from teenage singer with Shai Hulud to pop-punk icon reflects a career defined by artistic growth and dedication.
