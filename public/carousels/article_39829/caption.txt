HEALTH STORIES:

Country music legend Dolly Parton passed away at 80 in Nashville after a short battle with cancer. Her representatives confirmed her death in a statement, noting she died surrounded by loved ones at the Vanderbilt-Ingram Cancer Center.

Parton had been hospitalized earlier this month for kidney and autoimmune issues. In a video shared on her Instagram, one of her nephews, Bryan Seaver, honored her final wishes by announcing her passing. She had also appeared via video link for a Dollywood event days before her death, joking about her health struggles.

In September, Parton postponed Las Vegas concerts due to health challenges, later acknowledging she had neglected her well-being while caring for her late husband, Carl Dean. She told People she was dealing with health issues she hadn’t prioritized during her caregiving role.

Her legacy includes the Imagination Library, which has donated millions of books, and her support for vaccine research. Colleagues like Julia Roberts praised her as a joyful and generous figure.

Parton’s passing marks the end of an era for country music. Her resilience and generosity left a lasting impact, reminding fans of the power of kindness and creativity in the face of adversity.
