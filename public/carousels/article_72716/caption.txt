BUSINESS & FINANCE:

Kroger reported second-quarter 2026 earnings with CEO Gregory S. Foran highlighting improved e-commerce performance, brand growth in health and wellness, and cost-saving initiatives. The company faced challenges from a cyclospora outbreak and high fuel prices, which impacted sales.

Sales growth slowed in the final period due to the cyclospora outbreak, costing approximately 35 basis points in total company ID without fuel. Identical sales without fuel rose 0.2%, though pharmacy revenue declined 140 basis points due to lower drug prices. The macroeconomic environment, including fuel prices over $4, contributed to industry-wide softness.

Adjusted earnings per diluted share reached $1.09, reflecting Kroger's ability to maintain profitability amid sales pressure. The company emphasized ongoing progress in shelf availability, with all-time highs achieved, and improved pickup order accuracy. E-commerce growth remains a key focus, with Kroger leveraging its physical store network for competitive advantage.

Kroger plans to continue simplifying promotions and enhancing value perception through its customer value plan, a multiyear initiative. The company also welcomed Emily DeMartino as chief people officer, signaling renewed focus on workplace culture and associate experience.

Kroger's strategy demonstrates how retailers can balance cost management with customer experience during economic uncertainty, turning operational improvements into sustained competitive advantages.
