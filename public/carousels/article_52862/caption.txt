LIFESTYLE STORIES:

Vogue’s Business Beauty Trend Tracker highlights the latest shifts in the beauty and wellness markets, spotlighting products and trends driving growth. The report, produced with Spate, identifies top brands, ingredients, and hashtags shaping consumer demand.

In haircare, searches reflect growing interest in science-backed solutions combining plant-based ingredients with targeted benefits. Products like redensyl serums and rice water hairsprays are gaining traction for their ability to strengthen, shine, and reduce frizz. Brands such as Labo-H and Bvlgari Beauty are capitalizing on consumer demand for premium, performance-driven options.

TikTok trends like #BabyShowerMakeup and #QuickCurls highlight the platform’s influence on shaping beauty routines. Hashtags such as #LakeHair and #AntioxidantSerum emphasize the role of social media in promoting niche, functional products.

Wellness is also seeing strong growth, with demand for targeted, convenient, and science-backed solutions. Dopamine patches, acupressure wristbands, and men’s fertility supplements are among the rising stars. Brands like Pure Genius Protein and Sparkle Wellness are meeting this demand with innovative, easy-to-use formats.

The bodycare category is evolving with a focus on targeted, skin-friendly solutions. AHA lotions, grape soap, and kojic acid treatments are gaining attention for their functional and sensory appeal. Brands like QV Skincare and Luna Daily are leading the charge in offering specialized, dermatologist-approved care.

The report underscores a broader shift toward personalized, science-backed beauty and wellness products that cater to specific concerns and lifestyles.
