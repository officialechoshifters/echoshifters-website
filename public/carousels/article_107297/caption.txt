SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY:

SpaceX achieved a historic milestone by launching its Starship rocket into low-Earth orbit for the first time, marking a major breakthrough in reusable rocket technology.

The 407-foot Starship-Super Heavy rocket lifted off from Boca Chica, Texas, at 8:48 a.m. EDT. The mission included deploying 26 next-generation Starlink satellites and successfully splashing down in the Pacific Ocean after three hours in orbit.

Construction crews are preparing facilities at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station and Kennedy Space Center to host future Starship missions. This follows SpaceX's shift from Falcon 9 to Starship as its primary launch vehicle in Brevard County.

Elon Musk declared the flight a success on X, and NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman praised the launch as "gorgeous" and "inspirational," emphasizing the potential for routine Starship missions.

The mission demonstrated SpaceX's ability to reach orbit and manage all phases safely, a critical step toward making crewed lunar and Mars missions feasible within the next decade.
