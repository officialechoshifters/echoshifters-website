TECH & MEDIA: Analyst Warns Alphabet (GOOG) Faces Two Major AI Problems; SpaceX (SPCX) a Better AI Bet?

Alphabet's AI division has seen a series of high-profile departures, sparking concerns about the company's ability to develop future AI models.

Gene Munster, managing partner at Deepwater Asset Management, warned that the brain drain could affect Alphabet's culture of innovation and its ability to compete in the rapidly evolving AI landscape. He noted that Gemini has started to slip in AI model rankings relative to competitors, while Grok is expected to break into the top three models as new versions are released.

In contrast, SpaceX's AI division has seen significant improvements, with the company boasting a 66% year-over-year increase in revenue from its Starlink connectivity business. Analysts believe that SpaceX's growth potential in AI could be substantial, with estimates suggesting $750 billion in annual revenue potential if the company can scale its AI compute capacity to 15 GW by 2027.

However, critics argue that SpaceX's aggressive expansion plans may not be sustainable, given the company's significant cash burn and reliance on a small number of customers. JPMorgan estimates that SpaceX would need to spend $200 billion in capital expenditures per year from 2027 to 2028 to reach its ambitious growth targets.

Ultimately, the fate of Alphabet's AI division hangs in the balance, while SpaceX appears to be well-positioned for long-term success in the rapidly evolving tech landscape.
