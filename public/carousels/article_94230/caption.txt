Presley Gerber, the 27-year-old son of Cindy Crawford and Rande Gerber, has died at a Los Angeles rehabilitation center, according to medical examiner records. The cause of death remains undisclosed.

Gerber, a professional model like his mother and sister Kaia, began his career at age 16 with a runway debut for Moschino. He later worked with major fashion houses including Burberry, Dolce & Gabbana, and Bottega Veneta. His father co-founded Casamigos tequila with George Clooney.

The family spokesperson requested privacy following the death, stating it was a 'very difficult and painful time.' Gerber had been open about mental health challenges and substance use, sharing his journey in public appearances and a 2023 podcast interview where he emphasized helping others through his experiences.

Kaia Gerber recently discussed her brother's public struggle with addiction in an August Vogue cover story. She described how his openness about his journey affected the family dynamic, noting that his candor helped others feel less isolated.

Gerber's advocacy for mental health awareness, including videos discussing his treatment and recovery, highlighted his commitment to breaking stigma around these issues. His death underscores ongoing challenges in addressing mental health and addiction within high-profile families.
