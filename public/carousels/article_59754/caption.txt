Andy Williams, 'The Butcher,' collapses during wrestling match, dies hours later

Andy Williams collapsed during a tag team match at an Enjoy Wrestling event in Pennsylvania, prompting the event's immediate cancellation.
Enjoy Wrestling confirmed Williams received immediate CPR and was rushed to the hospital, but he later passed away.
Williams was a founding member of the metalcore band Every Time I Die and a professional wrestler with All Elite Wrestling.
AEW and fans mourn the loss of Williams, who left a legacy in both music and wrestling.
